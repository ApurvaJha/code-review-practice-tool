# ADR 023: Actionable Competency Frameworks

**Date:** 2026-07-30
**Author:** Apurva Jha
**Status:** Accepted

## Context
The initial engineering leadership frameworks provided in the UI modal were overly abstract. While conceptually sound, they lacked step-by-step structural guidance and conversational templates, making it difficult for engineers newer to the Engineering Manager persona to practically apply them to ambiguous scenarios.

## Decision
Overhaul the framework modal content in `product-thinking.html` to focus on concrete, step-by-step mental models.
1. Replace theoretical concepts with actionable blueprints: *The Problem-to-MVP Pipeline*, *The "Trade-off Triangle" Pivot*, and *The 70/20/10 Resource Rule*.
2. Include explicit "How to answer" scripts/templates for each competency so candidates have a reliable baseline structure to fall back on when navigating high-pressure interview scenarios.

## Consequences
*   **Positive:** Dramatically lowers the barrier to entry for formulating strong product-leadership responses. Candidates now have a direct blueprint for structuring their essays and verbal follow-ups.
*   **Negative:** None.
